In \texttt{\textcolor{cmd}{task} \textcolor{sub}{info} 6}, \texttt{6} is the task's \emph{ID}, the number beside it in the list. In \texttt{\textcolor{cmd}{task} \textcolor{attr}{ow:bot} \textcolor{sub}{ready}}, \texttt{\textcolor{attr}{ow:bot}} is a \emph{filter} that selects tasks assigned to any bot, and \texttt{\textcolor{sub}{ready}} is a \emph{report} that shows which of those tasks can be started.

\section*{Find and inspect tasks}

\begin{flushleft}
\texttt{\textcolor{cmd}{task}} or \texttt{\textcolor{cmd}{task} \textcolor{sub}{next}}\quad Lists the most urgent pending tasks. \\
\texttt{\textcolor{cmd}{task} \textcolor{sub}{list}}\quad Shows the standard report for pending tasks. \\
\texttt{\textcolor{cmd}{task} \textcolor{sub}{blocked}}\quad Shows tasks waiting on another task. \\
\texttt{\textcolor{cmd}{task} \textcolor{sub}{ready}}\quad Shows ready tasks assigned to \texttt{alf} by default. \\
\texttt{\textcolor{cmd}{task} \textcolor{sub}{summary}}\quad Shows pending counts and completion by project. \\
\texttt{\textcolor{cmd}{task} 6}\quad Shows the shell's short view of task 6. \\
\texttt{\textcolor{cmd}{task} \textcolor{sub}{info} 6}\quad Shows the full details and history of task 6. \\
\end{flushleft}

A filter by itself uses the default \texttt{\textcolor{sub}{next}} report. \texttt{\textcolor{attr}{ow:}} selects by owner and \texttt{\textcolor{attr}{pro:}} selects by project.

\begin{flushleft}
\texttt{\textcolor{cmd}{task} \textcolor{attr}{ow:alf}}\quad Selects tasks assigned to \texttt{alf}. \\
\texttt{\textcolor{cmd}{task} \textcolor{attr}{pro:radio}}\quad Selects tasks in \texttt{radio} and its subprojects. \\
\texttt{\textcolor{cmd}{task} \textcolor{attr}{pro.has:lesson}}\quad Selects tasks in projects with \texttt{lesson} in their names. \\
\texttt{\textcolor{cmd}{task} \textcolor{attr}{ow:bot} \textcolor{sub}{ready}}\quad Shows ready tasks assigned to any bot. \\
\texttt{\textcolor{cmd}{task} \textcolor{attr}{ow:claude} \textcolor{sub}{ready}}\quad Shows only Claude's ready tasks. \\
\end{flushleft}

\section*{Add and change tasks}

\begin{flushleft}
\texttt{\textcolor{cmd}{task} \textcolor{sub}{add} \textcolor{attr}{pro:para} \textcolor{desc}{'Review the draft'}}\quad Adds a task owned by \texttt{alf} by default. \\
\texttt{\textcolor{cmd}{task} \textcolor{sub}{add} \textcolor{attr}{ow:bot} \textcolor{attr}{depends:3} \textcolor{desc}{'Revise draft'}}\quad Adds a task that waits for task 3. \\
\texttt{\textcolor{cmd}{task} \textcolor{sub}{modify} 6 \textcolor{attr}{pro:para} \textcolor{attr}{ow:claude}}\quad Changes the project and owner of task 6. \\
\texttt{\textcolor{cmd}{task} \textcolor{sub}{modify} 6 /old/new/}\quad Replaces the first \texttt{old} with \texttt{new} in the description. \\
\texttt{\textcolor{cmd}{task} \textcolor{sub}{annotate} 6 \textcolor{desc}{'Check the examples'}}\quad Adds a note to task 6. \\
\texttt{\textcolor{cmd}{task} \textcolor{sub}{start} 6}\quad Marks task 6 active and records the start time. \\
\texttt{\textcolor{cmd}{task} \textcolor{sub}{stop} 6}\quad Clears the start time. Task 6 stays pending. \\
\texttt{\textcolor{cmd}{task} \textcolor{sub}{done} 6}\quad Marks task 6 complete. \\
\texttt{\textcolor{cmd}{task} \textcolor{sub}{delete} 6}\quad Asks to confirm deletion of task 6. \\
\end{flushleft}

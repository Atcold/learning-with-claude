# Radio

My eight stations in two places: in the Mac's terminal through mpv, and on the iPhone through a page that AlfDROID serves three minutes behind live, so a subway dropout plays through from the phone's buffer. The lesson covers the station titles and the rules both players share, the terminal player, the droid's relays and tapes, why the iPhone plays HLS (with the measurements that decided it), the page, and the `radio` command.

- `lesson.tex`: the lesson.

# What is Whisper Dictation?

Whisper Dictation is the menu-bar helper that types my speech into whatever app I'm focused on: I press ⌃⌥D, speak, press it again, and the transcript arrives as if I had typed it. This lesson answers the question I finally asked while debugging it today: what exactly is the `WhisperDictation.app` sitting in `~/.local/share`, why is it an app bundle rather than a script, and why does the Accessibility panel demand re-approval after every rebuild.
